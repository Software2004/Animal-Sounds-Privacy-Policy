Waypoint 01 / 09

Introduction

Animal Sounds & Ringtones (WildTones) ("the app," "we," "us") lets you browse animal sounds and ringtones, view animal wallpapers, save favorites, choose your language, and optionally subscribe to Premium to remove ads and unlock unlimited content. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored on your device, what information may be collected by integrated third‑party services, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and is effective as of 28 July 2026. By installing or using Animal Sounds & Ringtones (WildTones), you agree to the practices described here.

Waypoint 02 / 09

Information we collect

The app itself collects no personally identifiable information. It stores a small amount of app data on your device, and Google services used for ads, consent, purchases, and remote configuration may collect data automatically as part of their normal operation.

On‑device preferences

We store onboarding completion status and your selected language (e.g., "en", "es") in app preferences on your device. This is not transmitted to a developer server.

Favorites

When you favorite an animal, we store a fixed animal identifier (e.g., "lion") and the time it was added in a local on‑device database. This is not user‑generated content and contains no personal information.

Advertising data

The free version includes ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device/app signals, and ad interaction data (impressions/clicks) to show and measure ads.

Consent choices

In supported regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent dialog for personalized ads. Your consent choice is stored locally and reported to Google to manage ad personalization.

Remote config & purchases

Firebase Remote Config may collect an app instance identifier and device/app metadata to deliver configuration. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, which handles purchase tokens and subscription state.

What we do not collect

The app has no user accounts or sign‑in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone access.

Waypoint 03 / 09

How we use information

  • App functionality — showing animal sounds and wallpapers, including premium‑only content where applicable.
  • Favorites — saving your favorite animals on your device so they persist between sessions.
  • Preferences — remembering your language selection and onboarding status.
  • Advertising — showing ads in the free tier through AdMob, subject to your consent choices where applicable.
  • Premium purchases — enabling subscriptions (Weekly/Yearly/Lifetime), restoring purchases, and removing ads when Premium is active.
  • Remote configuration — using Firebase Remote Config to adjust app behavior (e.g., feature flags) without requiring a full app update.

Waypoint 04 / 09

Data sharing

We don't sell your information. The app uses a small number of third‑party services for ads, consent management, remote configuration, and billing. These providers may collect data as described in their own policies.

Google AdMob

Serves ads in the free tier and measures ad performance. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google UMP

Shows a consent dialog for ad personalization in supported regions and reports your consent status to Google. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Remote Config

Delivers configuration values that can change app behavior without an update. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

Processes subscriptions and manages purchase state. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not by us. Governed by Google Play Terms.

Glide

Loads wallpaper images efficiently. Glide itself does not add tracking or analytics beyond standard image fetching.

Each provider only receives the data needed to perform its function. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Waypoint 05 / 09

Data storage

Favorites and app preferences are stored locally on your device (in a local database and app preferences). The app does not use a custom backend or developer server.

Android Auto Backup is enabled, which means your favorites and preferences may be included in your device's Google backup (depending on your backup settings) and restored if you reinstall.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes this locally stored data from the device.

Waypoint 06 / 09

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. In all cases, the app gives you direct, practical control:

Manage favorites by adding or removing items in the Favorites screen.

Delete local app data by clearing storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Control ad personalization via the in‑app consent dialog (where shown) and Android ad settings.

Change language at any time from the in‑app language screen.

Manage subscriptions through Google Play and use the in‑app restore option for eligible purchases.

Waypoint 07 / 09

Children's privacy

Animal Sounds & Ringtones (WildTones) is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll take appropriate action.

Waypoint 08 / 09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page, and — where appropriate — an in‑app notice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
